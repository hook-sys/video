# Reference playbook — 12 SaaS promos (uploaded Oct 9, 2026)

What premium SaaS motion promos actually do, measured and watched frame by
frame. `ref-NN.jpg` are contact sheets (one frame every ~2.5–4 s);
`metrics.json` the measured numbers. This is data to learn from, not a
template: nothing here is copied, every video still varies.

`cuts` = hard cuts; `motion` = mean frame change; `still share` = frames
that barely move; `dark share` = frames with a dark background.

Two families, never mixed in one video:
- **Cut-driven** (01, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09): a cut every 1.5–3.3 s, type
  and UI alternate, energy carried by cuts.
- **One continuous camera** (02, 04, 10, 11, 12): almost no cuts (shots of
  7–34 s); the camera glides between things that are all on one big
  canvas; elements morph into the next scene. Calmer, more "premium".

Light wins: 9 of 12 are mostly light. Dark is used whole-video (04) or as
the "problem" half (01, 07). Saturation is low in most (0.03–0.26): white
or pale backgrounds, ONE accent colour (orange, mint, blue, teal) as soft
glows/blobs, not as flat fills.

## Patterns that repeat (what makes them premium)

1. **One idea per beat, few words.** Most frames carry 1–6 words. Long
   sentences only appear as UI content, never as the headline.
2. **The key phrase gets a container**: a pill / highlight box behind one
   phrase (09, 03, 06), or one word in the accent colour (10: "MSP
   Operator", 12: "build").
3. **Type that is animated by letter**: typed (07, 09, 12), letters
   revealing from blur (12), wide-tracked single word (09), word-by-word
   with inline icons (03).
4. **Real UI is the hero**, cropped and zoomed into ONE control at a time,
   with a cursor that clicks (01, 02, 05, 11, 10). UI floats as white glass
   with a soft shadow, often tilted or on a curved surface.
5. **A label above the UI** naming the feature: AI badge + word (10), layer
   name (12), side caption (09).
6. **Problem → reveal → features → proof → end** structure in all twelve;
   the problem uses chaos (node web, crossed chips, spinners, "?" bars,
   scattered panels), the reveal uses order (one logo, one ring).
7. **Orbit / ring of brands or apps** around the product (06, 07, 10, 11).
8. **Zoom out to a grid** of many screens, then into one KPI (08, 11).
9. **One soft glow colour** drifting at the edges (09 orange, 10 mint, 11
   coral/mint, 12 blue) — the background is never flat and never busy.
10. **Success moment**: confetti card (01), number change (08), "16X" pill
    (12).
11. **Time collapse**: repeated, blurred word ("Weeks Weeks Weeks", 12),
    "Day 1 → Day 6 → Months" (10).
12. **Ending**: logo + one button (pill, accent colour) + URL, sometimes a
    cursor clicking the button. Never a paragraph.

caption, around, between, inline), cards, chips, devices, stat, compare,
flow, chart, logo + button end, depth/journey camera languages, glass
